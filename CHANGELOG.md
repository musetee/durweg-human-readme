# Changelog

## 2026-11-01: the rules live in this workflow

- The rules for READMEs and other docs for people are now part of this workflow:
  [`skills/human-readme/guide.md`](skills/human-readme/guide.md) and
  [`guide_cn.md`](skills/human-readme/guide_cn.md). Before, the author kept them in a separate,
  private place; they were cleaned up for publishing (examples from private projects replaced by
  the Durweg README, or left out).
- New rule: one line of contents under the README's title, linking every `##` section.
- New rule: when there are several ways to install, say first that they are alternatives (a
  "pick one" table, Option A / Option B), so they do not read as steps in a row. Added after
  the two install subsections of the Durweg README were read as two steps in a row; the author
  decided to keep it as a general rule.
- The guide opens with a checklist; step 6 checks the README against it.
- Steps 3, 6 and 7 of the skill point at the guide; step 7 changes the rules here and records
  the reason in this file.
